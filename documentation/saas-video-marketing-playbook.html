Why Do Most SaaS Video Marketing Campaigns Fail to Drive Conversions?

Most B2B SaaS brands invest thousands in video production only to see minimal lifts in demo requests or free trial sign-ups.   • Using overly technical jargon that alienates non-technical decision-makers

  • Failing to include social proof or customer use cases to build trust
