What Are the Most Common SaaS Explainer Video Editing Mistakes to Avoid?

Even with great raw footage, poor editing choices can tank your explainer video performance. Our team helps clients avoid these common pitfalls:

  • Leading with your logo or product name instead of a pain point: 80% of viewers drop off in the first 3 seconds of explainer videos that lead with branding instead of speaking to a problem they’re actively trying to solve.
  • Overloading viewers with too much information: A 60-second explainer should focus on 1-2 core value props, not a full list of every feature your product offers.
  • Using generic stock footage that doesn’t match your target audience: A B2B SaaS tool for construction managers will perform far better with footage of actual job sites than generic office stock clips.
  • Skipping clear CTAs: Viewers won’t take action if you don’t tell them exactly what to do next, and where to click.
