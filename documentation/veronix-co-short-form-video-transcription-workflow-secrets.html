Why Is Automated Video Transcription Critical for High-Performing Short-Form Content?

Recent social media data shows 85% of short-form video is watched on mute, making accurate, well-timed captions a non-negotiable for retention and algorithm performance. But transcription delivers value far beyond just captions: it turns hours of raw interview, vlog, or event footage into a searchable document, so editors can jump directly to the most engaging soundbites, quotes, or moments without rewatching entire files. Veronix Co’s in-house testing has found that transcription-first workflows cut total editing time by up to 70% for most client projects, with no drop in final content quality.
